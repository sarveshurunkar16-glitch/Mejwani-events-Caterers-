About Us

Welcome to Mejwani Events & Catering, your premier destination for pure veg community events, workshops, and gatherings. As a dedicated pure veg center, we specialize in vegetarian and vegan-friendly spaces for learning, connecting, and celebrating. Located in the heart of Nandani, Shirol Road, Taluka Shirol, District Kolhapur, we offer a vibrant, wholesome environment with top-notch facilities tailored to your needs. Whether you're planning a family event, a corporate meeting, or a cultural program, Mejwani Events & Catering ensures a memorable experience with pure veg catering and ambiance.

All programs accepted, featuring our expert pure veg chef! We cater to every type of event with delicious, wholesome vegetarian cuisine prepared by our skilled chef, ensuring a delightful experience for all guests.
